# Golden financial fixtures — V1 correctness gate

This folder is the executable, reviewable financial truth table for BizLens.
The fixtures in `tests/fixtures/golden/` are **synthetic** and **hand audited**.
They are deliberately *not* generated by the same code under test.

Each case consists of a CSV file and a separately reviewed
`.expected.json` contract. The expected values are intentionally small
enough for a developer to recompute by hand. Do **not** use an automated
snapshot update tool to overwrite these expectations after a code change.

## Case 1: `order_lines` — one order, multiple sales lines

Six CSV rows represent **four orders** (A, B, C, D); A and C contain two
lines. Each line supplies its own revenue, cost and discount amount.

| Measurement | Hand calculation | Expected |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Revenue | 125 + 75 + 100 + 150 + 50 + 200 | 700.00 |
| Cost | 50 + 30 + 65 + 120 + 30 + 80 | 375.00 |
| Gross profit | 700 − 375 | 325.00 |
| Gross margin | (325 / 700) × 100 | 46.43% |
| Discount | 5 + 0 + 10 + 20 + 0 + 15 | 50.00 |
| Orders | unique(A, B, C, D) | 4 |
| Average order value | 700 / 4 | 175.00 |

Under the current **chronological-midpoint** split (not calendar quarters),
Jan 1–2 is previous, Jan 3–4 is current:

- Previous: revenue 300, cost 145, profit 155, margin 51.67%, two orders.
- Current: revenue 400, cost 230, profit 170, margin 42.50%, two orders.
- Margin declines by **9.17 percentage points**; cost and discount
  pressures exist, while total profit still grows by 15.

The expected diagnoses are **Margin Decline**, **Cost Pressure** and
**Discount Pressure**; there is no low-margin product meeting the product
concentration threshold.

## Case 2: `profit_reversal` — revenue growth conceals profit collapse

Four orders across four days, with low-cost P1 in the first period and
low-margin P2 in the second:

| Measurement | Hand calculation | Expected |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Revenue | 100 + 100 + 150 + 150 | 500.00 |
| Cost | 30 + 30 + 145 + 145 | 350.00 |
| Gross profit | 500 − 350 | 150.00 |
| Gross margin | (150 / 500) × 100 | 30.00% |
| Discount | 5 + 5 + 10 + 20 | 40.00 |
| Orders | unique(A, B, C, D) | 4 |
| Average order value | 500 / 4 | 125.00 |

- Previous: revenue 200, cost 60, profit 140, gross margin 70%.
- Current: revenue 300, cost 290, profit 10, gross margin 3.33%.
- Revenue **increases 50%** while profit **falls 92.86%**.
- Cost grows **383.33%**; discount grows **200%**.
- P2 contributes **60% of total revenue**, with **3.33% gross margin**.

This must produce five distinct, evidence-backed diagnoses, including
**Revenue Up / Profit Down** and **High Revenue / Low Profit Product**.

## Executable rules

The parametrized tests in `tests/metrics/test_financial_golden.py` verify:

1. **Exact** financial output against independently recorded expected values.
2. Period balances and unique order grain.
3. Diagnoses, severity, and selected numeric evidence.
4. `what_changed` numbers and directions.
5. Replay step data matching the same calculation and report objects.
6. Conservation of revenue, cost, profit, discount and orders across periods.
7. Row-order independence: shuffling rows cannot change business facts.

The rest of the metrics and confidence regression suites cover invalid data,
missing fields and severity independence. These golden fixtures specifically
protect positive, high-signal business stories.

**Limitations:** Floating-point sums currently round to two decimals, and
periods remain split at a midpoint. This suite does not certify tax-grade
accounting, refund normalization, currency handling, or the **quarterly**
charts promised by the V1 experience charter. Those require separate
domain decisions and precision/period test suites.
